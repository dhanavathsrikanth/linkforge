# PivotUrl User Guide

## What is PivotUrl?

PivotUrl turns long, ugly URLs into short, clean ones you can share anywhere — social media, emails, text messages, QR codes, or your link-in-bio page. You can also password-protect links, send phone users to different pages, run A/B tests, auto-fill previews with AI, and ask questions about your clicks in plain English.

---

## Your First Link

1. Log in.
2. On the Links page, paste a long URL in the top bar.
3. Optionally type a short name (slug), or leave it blank.
4. Hit Enter.
5. Your short link is copied to your clipboard.

---

## The Links Page

**Top bar:** Paste a URL and hit Enter for a quick link.  
**Table:** Every link you've made. Shows slug, where it goes, title, clicks, and action icons.  
**Action icons on each row:** Copy, visit, view analytics, make a QR code, edit, delete.  
**Toolbar buttons:** Bulk Create, Export CSV, Check Links.

To edit a link, click the row to expand it.

---

## Making a Link with Extra Options

Click **Create Link** to open the side panel.

### General
- **Destination URL** (required) — where people end up.
- **Slug** — the short bit after your domain. Click the sparkles icon for an AI suggestion.
- **Title** — a label to help you find it. Click **Auto-fill** to have AI make one up from the destination page.
- **Description / OG Image** — what shows up when you share on social media. Auto-filled by AI.
- **Tags** — labels to group links. Press Enter or comma to add.

### UTM Parameters
Tracking tags for Google Analytics. These get added to your link automatically when someone visits.

| Field | Tracks | Example |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Source | Where traffic came from | `newsletter`, `twitter` |
| Medium | How it got there | `email`, `social` |
| Campaign | Promotion name | `summer-sale` |
| Term | Paid keyword | `running-shoes` |
| Content | Which ad version | `hero-banner` |

### A/B Testing
Test different destinations to see which works better.

1. Turn A/B testing on.
2. Add at least 2 variants. Each has a URL and a weight (what % of traffic it gets).
3. The coloured bar shows the split.
4. After enough clicks, click **AI Optimize** to get a better split suggested by AI.

Needs the Growth plan.

### Advanced
- **Password** — lock the link. Visitors see a password page first.
- **Expiration** — make the link stop working on a date or after a set number of clicks.
- **iOS Destination** — iPhone/iPad users go here.
- **Android Destination** — Android users go here.
- **Open Graph** — title, description, preview image for shares.

## AI Tools

### Auto-Fill
Click **Auto-fill** next to Title. AI reads the page and fills in the title, description, and preview image all at once.

### Slug Suggestions
Click the sparkles icon next to the slug box. The system makes a readable slug from the URL.

### Check Links
Click **Check Links** on the Links page. Each link is tested. If a page has changed, AI flags it. Results show how many links are fine, broken, or changed.

### Ask AI
In any link's analytics view, find the "Ask AI about this link" box. Type questions like:
- "Which device got the most clicks?"
- "How many visitors this week?"
- "What's my top referrer?"

AI reads your numbers and answers.

---

## Bulk Create

Click **Bulk Create** on the Links page.

**Paste URLs:** Type one URL per line, add shared tags/UTMs, review, create.

**Upload CSV:** Make a spreadsheet with columns like `destination`, `slug`, `title`. Upload it. The system figures out which column is which. Review, then create.

Needs the Growth plan.

---

## Export CSV

Click **Export CSV** to download all your links as a spreadsheet. Includes slug, destination, title, tags, clicks, UTMs, and creation date.

---

## QR Codes

Click the QR icon on a link row. Change colours. Download as a picture. QR scans show up in analytics.

---

## Analytics

Click the chart icon on any link row.

**Quick numbers:** Total clicks, unique visitors, today's clicks, top device, top country.

**Charts:** Clicks per day (last 7 days), device breakdown, top countries, top referrers.

**Live Activity:** A live feed of recent clicks that updates every 5 seconds. Shows device, browser, location, and referrer for the last 50 clicks.

---

## What Happens When Someone Clicks Your Link

1. They click your short link.
2. If it has a password, they see a password page (good for 24 hours after entering it).
3. If A/B testing is on, they get a random variant.
4. If they're on a phone with a special link set, they get that.
5. Tracking tags are added.
6. They get redirected.
7. The click is saved for your analytics.

All under a second.
